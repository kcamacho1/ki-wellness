Information We Collect

Ki Wellness collects information you provide directly to us to deliver personalized health tracking and insights.

Account Information

Username, email address, name, age, weight, height, and health goals

Health Data

Food intake, water consumption, mood tracking, and personal notes

Usage Data

How you interact with our application and features

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our health tracking services.

  • Provide and maintain the Ki Wellness service
  • Process your food journal entries and health tracking data
  • Personalize your experience and provide relevant insights
  • Send you important updates about our service
  • Improve our application and develop new features
3

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except in the following limited circumstances:

Limited Exceptions

  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and safety
  • With your explicit permission

Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Encryption

Data encrypted in transit and at rest

Authentication

Secure access controls and authentication

Monitoring

Regular security assessments and updates

Access Control

Limited access on need-to-know basis

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through your account settings or by contacting our support team.

Access your personal information

Export your health data

Third-Party Services

Our application integrates with external APIs (Open Food Facts, USDA) to provide comprehensive nutritional information. These services have their own privacy policies that govern how they handle data.

8

Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and provide core functionality. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes or sell your data to third-party advertisers.

9

Children's Privacy

Ki Wellness is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
