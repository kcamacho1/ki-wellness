My Journey of Transformation

From darkness to light, from struggle to strength - here's how I found my way to wellness and how I can help you find yours.

The Dark Years (10 Years)

I spent a decade battling alcoholism, struggling with benzodiazepines and painkillers. My life was consumed by anxiety, depression, and chronic lower back pain. I felt trapped in a cycle of self-destruction, unable to see a way out.

The Turning Point

Six years ago, I made the courageous decision to change my life. I committed to sobriety and began exploring holistic approaches to healing. It wasn't easy, but I discovered that true wellness comes from balancing mind, body, and spirit.

The Transformation

Today, I'm clean, healthy, and passionate about helping others find their path to wellness. I've learned that recovery isn't just about stopping harmful behaviors - it's about creating a life worth living through holistic health practices.
