My Journey of Transformation

I am not a stranger to difficult and sometimes dark struggles. Here's how I found my way to wellness and how I can guide you in matters of personal health, fitness, recovery, nutrition, sleep, and overall well-being.

The Dark Years (10 Years)

I spent a decade battling alcoholism, struggling with benzodiazepines and painkillers. My life was consumed by anxiety, depression, and chronic lower back pain. I understand the depths of struggle and the feeling of being trapped in cycles that seem impossible to break.

The Turning Point

Six years ago, I made the courageous decision to change my life. I committed to sobriety and began exploring holistic approaches to healing. Through this journey, I discovered that true wellness comes from balancing mind, body, and spirit - and I learned how to guide others through similar transformations.

The Transformation

Today, I'm clean, healthy, and passionate about guiding others in their wellness journey. I can direct and support you in matters related to exercise, fitness, recovery, nutrition, sleep, and overall personal health. My experience has taught me that true wellness comes from creating sustainable, balanced practices that honor your unique needs.
