Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I run out of questions?

When you reach your monthly limit, you can either wait for the next billing cycle or purchase additional question credits to continue using AI features immediately.

Can I switch between plans?

Yes! You can upgrade to a monthly subscription at any time, or use pay-as-you-go credits alongside your subscription. Your subscription will automatically use monthly questions first, then credits.

Do question credits expire?

No, question credits never expire. You can use them whenever you need them, even if you switch between plans.

What counts as an AI question?

Each AI-powered analysis, chat interaction, or pattern recognition counts as one question. Questions are limited to 1,500 tokens (input + output) for cost control.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your monthly subscription at any time through your account settings. You'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.
