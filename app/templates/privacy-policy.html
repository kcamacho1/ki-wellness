🔒 Privacy Policy

At Ki Wellness, your privacy and trust are sacred. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. What We Collect

  • Name, email, and basic profile details (e.g., age, preferences)
  • Wellness data you choose to share (e.g., food logs, goals, moods)
  • Anonymous app usage data to improve user experience

2. How We Use Your Info

  • To provide personalized coaching and AI suggestions
  • To track progress and help you meet your wellness goals
  • To contact you about appointments, updates, or improvements

3. Who Can Access Your Data

Only you and, if requested, your assigned human coach. We do not sell your data. If we ever share anonymized insights for health research, we’ll ask first or compensate you fairly.

4. Your Rights

  • Request access to or deletion of your data anytime
  • Opt out of marketing emails
  • Pause or delete your account from your settings

5. Data Security

We use secure cloud storage, HTTPS encryption, and strong authentication to protect your information. Your wellness is private—and it stays that way.

6. Contact

Questions or concerns? Reach out any time: Email me

This policy was last updated July 2025. Ki Wellness may revise it as we grow—always with your well-being in mind.
