🌿 About Ki Wellness

Ki Wellness exists to make holistic health accessible, affordable, and empowering for everyone. Whether you prefer working with a compassionate human health coach or our friendly AI assistant, you'll find tools and support to nurture mind, body, and spirit.

💡 Why We Exist

We believe everyone deserves the tools to feel well, no matter their income or background. Our platform combines AI-powered suggestions with the option to work with real coaches when you need personal guidance.

Subscriptions help fund free access for those in need, research into better health practices, and the vision of creating a retreat center for rest, learning, and community care.

✨ App Features ✨

  • 📊 Track meals and nutrition with your Food Journal
  • 📈 Visualize progress with helpful graphs
  • 🥗 Plan meals that fit your lifestyle
  • 🧠 Get AI prompts, suggestions, or analysis each month
  • 🙋‍♀️ Connect with human coaches anytime
